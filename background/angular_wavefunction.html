Angular Wavefunction

The point of this page is to derive relationships involving the angular component of the rovibrational wavefunction, \(\ket{J,K,M}\).

\(\ket{J,K,M}\) is an eigenstate of \(\jop^2\), and the molecule-fixed z-component of the angular momentum operator, \(\jop_z\) with eigenvalues:

\[\begin{split}\jop^2\ket{J,K,M} =& \hbar^2 J(J+1)\ket{J,K,M}\\ \jop_z\ket{J,K,M} =& \hbar K\ket{J,K,M}.\end{split}\]

We additionally note that \(\jop^2 = \jop_x^2 + \jop_y^2 + \jop_z^2\).

The molecule-fixed components of \(\jvec\) obey the anomalous commutation relation \(\left[\jop_\alpha, \jop_\beta\right] = -i\hbar \jop_\gamma\). As a result \(\jop_\pm = \jop_x \pm i\jop_y\) lower and raise \(K\) respectively. Note that the sign of the commutator and the function of the ladder operators are the reverse of the space-fixed case.

The action of the operators on the angular momentum eigenstates are given by: .. math:

\jop^2\ket{J,K} =& \hbar^2 J(J+1)\ket{J,K}\\
\jop_z\ket{J,K} =& \hbar K\ket{J,K}\\
\jop_\pm\ket{J,K} =& \hbar C^\mp_{J,K}\ket{J,K\mp1}

where:

\[C^\pm_{J,K} =& \sqrt{J(J+1) - K(K\pm1)}\]
